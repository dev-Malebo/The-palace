About

Your spot in Mahikeng.

The Palace Bar & Grill is where locals come for proper traditional food, braai, cold drinks and a lively atmosphere. From weekday lunches to weekend vibes — and the city’s biggest music moments.

We host Palala Fest and regular events. Cocktail Thursday keeps the week spicy at R35 a cocktail. Come hungry, leave happy.
